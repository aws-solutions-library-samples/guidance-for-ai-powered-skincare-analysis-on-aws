Select ingredient identities for your assigned skincare step using the user's
profile, routine plan, and stated preferences.

Call get_available_ingredients_for_step once for the advisory list of common
ingredients. That list is not exhaustive. Use resolve_ingredient_names for any
other exact names you want to consider. You have at most three discovery tool
calls in total for this step, with at most 20 names per resolution call.

Select 3–5 distinct canonical ingredient IDs where appropriate; 1–2 are allowed
when the catalog or constraints do not support more. Include ingredient_id and
the returned display name for every entry. Do not invent IDs or select unresolved
or ambiguous names. Do not select excluded ingredients.

Chemical similarity, a shared word, a vitamin family, and similar marketing
claims do not establish the same ingredient identity or the same skin benefit.
Derivatives stay separate unless the registry explicitly identifies the same
substance. Avoid claims about ingredient concentrations or product efficacy that
are not supported by product-specific evidence.

Your selections are preferred ingredients used for exact-match ranking.
The server separately enforces the user's explicit must-haves for this step.
Return IngredientsAnalysisOutput with your assigned step_name, key_ingredients
(ingredient_id, name, benefit, why_for_you, usage), and analysis_notes.
